# Credits

## NASA audio (web/nasa/)

The tower communications use short excerpts of public-domain NASA recordings
(audio highlight reels and sound bites digitised by the Houston Audio Control
Room, NASA Johnson Space Center; source: https://archive.org/details/NasaAudioHighlightReels).
They are normalised to mono 22 kHz MP3 and are not otherwise altered.
NASA is the source of the material. This game is not endorsed by NASA and
nothing here implies NASA endorsement (NASA media usage guidelines). Recognisable
voices appear in some clips (astronauts, flight controllers, President Kennedy).
